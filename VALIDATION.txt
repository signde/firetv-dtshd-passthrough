Persistent package v0.1.1, 2026-10-06
Target: Gazelle PS7702.4965N, Magisk30.2. Separate from the Dolby module.

v0.1.0 boot/restart supervision worked, and DD/DD+ playback passed, but DTS
playback failed before first packing. Its wrapper let the native CModule be
collected, leaving function pointers into unmapped executable memory.
Reproduced in a disposable ARM32 process: unretained native reset faults after
collection; retained native reset succeeds. No live audio used by that test.

v0.1.1 retains the CModule explicitly. After leaving the same setup scope and
forcing garbage collection, the actual captured Emby raw stream repacks to
19,267,584 bytes with SHA256:
32d0304b679d60dfa33667a342a4ce85b8eebfbc81d0c018dbb0c52f607252bf
for input chunks7,2012,131072. This exactly matches the validated reference.
Startup now forces GC and tests the native functions before announcing ACTIVE.
The native C packer and established transport/timing logic are unchanged.

v0.1.1 installation and reboot activation passed on HAL425. Startup reported
nativeAfterGc=true before ACTIVE. Installed payload hashes all pass, HDMI mode
reads6, SELinux remains enforcing, and the separate Dolby module hashes are
unchanged. Final audible AVR playback validation PASSED: user confirms Emby,
Nova and Kodi correctly play DD, DD+, DTS-HD MA and DTS:X with v0.1.1 active.
The saved boot-session log records four patched HD stream lifetimes, thirteen
discontinuity resets, four matching transport closures/timing restorations/owner
cleanups, and no trial-error or fatal events. HAL425 remained active throughout.
App and format identities are user-observed, not inferred from HAL events.
No audio bytes were captured by the installed module.
The earlier temporary prototype passed eight HBR sessions and12 seek/resume
resets in Nova/Emby; this does not substitute for packaged playback verification.
